About Finom Healthcare

Finom Healthcare Private Limited is an Indian pharmaceutical and healthcare company engaged in the wholesale trading of pharmaceutical and medical products. Established in 2023, the company aims to build meaningful relationships across the healthcare ecosystem through professional service, responsible business practices and a commitment to supporting healthcare needs.
